When analyzing the diversity of bacterial populations it is important to consider a potential sampling bias that can distort your pangenome analysis. Our tool PhyloThin is a coalescent-theory-based approach to identify prokaryotic genomes that can be considered as oversampled.

For access to PhyloThin, check out our [github repository](https://github.com/fbaumdicker/phylothin).
